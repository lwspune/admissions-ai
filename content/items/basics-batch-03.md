## 1. The 39 moves from class 6 to class 5 (please confirm: these are shipped items)

An admit to class 6 draws basics tagged up to class 5, and until now nothing was tagged below 6. I
read every class 6 basics item as a child who has just finished class 5 (on *Maths Mela* 5 and
*Our Wondrous World* 5) and moved the ones that child can fairly do. **A move to 5 only opens an
item to admits to class 6; admits to 7–12 draw everything up to 6 and see no change.** It is
undone by setting the tag back.

**Left at class 6, on purpose:** primes, HCF and LCM and comparing unlike fractions (*Ganita
Prakash* 6; *Maths Mela* 5 ch. 13 starts common multiples, but the bank's LCM word problems go
further, so the later class stands), degrees and clock angles, perimeter, magnets (*Curiosity* 6), S-D-45 (the clay pot:
the class 5 book says a matka keeps water cool but not why), and English with words such as

With the moves and this batch, an admit to class 6 can draw (easy / moderate / difficult): maths
8 / 3 / 4, English 12 / 5 / 6, science 7 / 3 / 3: every stratum at least twice what a paper needs.
